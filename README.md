# Pete's Pain

Live: https://ploug76.github.io/petes-pain/  ·  Free sample: https://ploug76.github.io/petes-pain/?sample=bhs

Load Pete, a crash-test dummy, into a giant slingshot and fling him into goofy smashable stages. Pete is a real Verlet ragdoll: he flops, bonks, bounces and tumbles. Tap mid-flight near a glowing ring to grab it, and tap again to let go.

- Sandbox stages (3 launches each; the props stay knocked over between launches): **City Plaza**, **Backyard BBQ**, **Construction Site**
- Mini-modes: **Pete Bowling** (3 frames, strikes and spares), **Highest Bounce** (super trampolines; your best height counts), **Target Smash** (12 targets in 3 launches)
- Scoring: ouch damage (head bonks count double), flips, airtime, bounces, grabs, smashed and knocked-over props, chain-reaction confetti crates, and targets. Hits close together build a COMBO multiplier (up to x5).
- Juice: screen shake, slow-mo on big hits, and quiet synthesized "oof" sounds with a mute toggle. There is no music.
- Phone-first, works in portrait and landscape. Text is at least 16px and buttons are at least 44px.

Original game inspired by the general idea of slingshot ragdoll games. It uses no names, art or assets from any other game.
Pete's likeness is a drawn pixel portrait (J Woo method). The real photo is never shipped.

Source/build: `/workspace/petes-pain-src` (`python3 build.py`; bump `src/version` on every push).
